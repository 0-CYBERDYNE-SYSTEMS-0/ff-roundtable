## 2️⃣ What the soil says

- **pH 6.3** → inside the 6.0–6.9 adequacy window for corn in Iowa loams (ISU PM 1688, rev. 2013). No lime needed; no pH-driven penalty on any N source.
- **OM 3.4%** → typical Des Moines Lobe silt loam; expect roughly 20–40 lb N/ac mineralized in-season. **Do not subtract this from the recommendation** — the MRTN database already reflects soils like this.
- **Silt loam, rain-fed, humid continental** → moderate nitrate-leaching risk on wet springs, denitrification risk if saturated. This is the core argument for **spring/sidedress timing over any pre-plant-heavy program** — and why I'll resist any large single early application.
- **⚠️ Common error to avoid:** the corn-after-soybean MRTN *already includes* the rotation effect. Do not subtract a "soybean credit" on top.
