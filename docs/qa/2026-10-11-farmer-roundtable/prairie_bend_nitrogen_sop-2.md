## Four dated planning tasks — dates are windows, not forecast
1. **2027-04-05 — Verify/order:** Confirm product-only cap, delivered quote, and N-FACT rate; reserve urea only if quote remains applicable.
2. **2027-04-12 — Preplant planning:** If applying urea, incorporate promptly or use a labeled urease inhibitor; proceed only when soil is trafficable and not saturated. Avoid placing concentrated fertilizer with seed.
3. **2027-04-19 — Planting check:** Record planted acres and product applied. Do not infer weather; adjust dates to field conditions.
4. **2027-05-17 — In-season review:** At appropriate crop stage, use Iowa State LSNT guidance/test and reassess need, remaining budget, and feasible application. Do not exceed $65/ac product spend without farmer approval.
