## Technical Notes

- **Tone:** Confident, reassuring, authoritative-but-approachable
- **Pacing:** Deliberate in problem section, energetic in solution section, triumphant in payoff
- **Music cues:** Ambient → electronic pulse → driving rhythm → uplifting swell → clean resolve
- **Voice:** Male or female, mid-30s to 50s, warm American accent (Midwest ideal for agricultural authenticity)
